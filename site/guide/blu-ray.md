# A Blu-ray disc

**BD-JB** runs its exploit from a Blu-ray disc you burn yourself. It needs a PS5 with a disc drive (a
Slim or a Pro with its drive attached counts), and nothing on the console is erased.

| Your firmware | The exploit after BD-JB |
|---|---|
| up to 7.61 | **UMTX2** |
| up to 12.00 | **Poopsploit** |

Its project: [BD-JB](https://github.com/Gezine/BD-JB-1250). No disc drive? Use [the web browser](browser.md)
or [the YouTube app](youtube.md). Not sure which fits? [Pick your firmware](get-started.md#pick).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Read the whole page before you start, then follow it in order. This guide doesn't host exploits: for
> the disc image and how to burn it, [BD-JB's own page](https://github.com/Gezine/BD-JB-1250) has the
> last word.

## What you need {#what-you-need}

- **A PS5 with a disc drive, on 12.00 or lower.**
- **A computer with a Blu-ray burner**, and software that burns a disc image.
- **A blank BD-R or BD-RE.** A BD-RE can be erased and burned again when a new version comes out.
- **BD-JB's disc image** for your firmware's exploit, from its project's page.
- **A computer or a phone on the same network**, for later: it copies Playtendoh to the PS5.

## Section I: Prep work {#prep}

1. Check your firmware once more: **Settings > System > System Software > Console Information**. It
   must be in the table above.
2. Turn off automatic updates, if you haven't: **Settings > System > System Software > System Software
   Update and Settings**. Turn off both downloading and installing update files automatically.
3. Close every game and app, then **Restart** the PS5 from the power menu (not rest mode). An exploit
   works best on a console that has just started.

## Section II: Burn the disc {#burn}

1. Download BD-JB's disc image for your firmware's exploit.
2. Burn it to the blank disc with the settings its page gives. A disc burned another way may not
   start.

## Section III: Run the exploit {#run}

Run the one for your firmware.

### UMTX2, up to 7.61 {#umtx2}

1. Put the disc in the PS5. It shows on the home screen as a disc does.
2. Start it, and don't press anything until it says it's done, or a notification says the HEN has
   loaded.

### Poopsploit, up to 12.00 {#poopsploit}

1. Put the disc in the PS5. It shows on the home screen as a disc does.
2. Start it, and don't press anything until it says it's done, or a notification says the HEN has
   loaded.

> [!TIP]
> **A kernel exploit doesn't always work the first time.** If the disc stops, the PS5 freezes or it
> restarts, nothing is harmed: restart the PS5 and start the disc again. It can take a few tries.

## Section IV: Load the HEN {#hen}

The disc loads a HEN once the exploit has run, or has you pick one, as BD-JB's page says. On 12.00 and
lower that's **etaHEN**, the HEN Playtendoh is tested with most.

Wait for the HEN's notification before you go on. The next page has each HEN, and the one setting
Playtendoh needs in it.

## After every restart {#again}

The jailbreak doesn't survive turning the PS5 off. After every full power-off: start the disc again,
load your HEN, then start Playtendoh. Rest mode keeps the jailbreak, so you can usually put the PS5 in
rest mode instead.
