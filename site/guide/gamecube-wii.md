# GameCube and Wii

The GameCube and the Wii play on Dolphin, in [Porpoise X](https://github.com/premohq/Porpoise-X), with
Playtendoh's own in-game menu.

## What you need {#what-you-need}

- **Your games**, dumped from your own discs.
- Nothing else: no BIOS, no keys.

## Your games {#games}

| Console | Folder | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| GameCube | **`/data/porpoise/games/GameCube`** | `.iso`, `.gcm`, `.rvz`, `.ciso`, `.gcz`, `.wia` |
| Wii | **`/data/porpoise/games/Wii`** | the same, and `.wbfs`, `.wad` |

Or choose any folder on each side's **Settings > Game files**. `.rvz` keeps a disc small and plays the
same.

> [!TIP]
> **Wii games bought on the Wii U's eShop** don't play on the Wii U side (Cemu doesn't run Wii games).
> Dumped as Wii games, they play here.

## Controllers {#controllers}

The DualSense plays both. Real Wii Remotes connect through a **DolphinBar** in a USB port. A GameCube
game that links to a **Game Boy Advance** can have one on the link cable.
